About KADR

We create visual stories with a refined, cinematic perspective.

KADR is a creative production team based in Riyadh that crafts high-quality photography, video, and brand content for ambitious businesses. We work closely with cafes, restaurants and brands to deliver polished work that feels premium and effortless.

Our approach blends strong visual direction with considered execution so your story reaches the right audience with clarity and impact.
